The second alpha of 0.5 adds companion protocol version 10: an app can ask a board to use the
most transmit power it may, and the board says when it has worked that power out for its board,
antenna and region.

It is an alpha. The protocol is a draft and will change, and a later build may not talk to this
one or keep its sessions. Nobody outside the project has reviewed the cryptography. Do not rely
on it for anything that matters.

## What changed

* **Maximum transmit power** (companion protocol version 10). An app can send `SET` power `-128`
  to ask the board to work out the highest power it may use. `SELF` reports the resulting power
  and whether it is the maximum the board worked out. An explicit power setting still uses exactly
  the power the app asks for, if the board permits it.
* **Settings apply together.** A region and a role sent together are kept and applied with one
  restart, rather than restarting after each setting.
* **Board support and setup fixes.** The node ports and board configuration have been updated for
  the current ESP32 and nRF52840 targets.

First contact, sessions, messages, groups, positions, cards and routing are unchanged on the air
from 0.5.0-alpha.1. A board on 0.5.0-alpha.1 can be updated over its companion link and keeps its
address, sessions, contacts, groups, messages, settings and paired phones.

## What was tested, and what was not

CI built every image of this release for every board and region and ran the firmware test suite,
including the companion protocol version 10 vectors. The images have not been exercised on every
physical board as part of this release.

## Updating

A Heltec V3 on 0.2.0-alpha.1 or later takes this release over the link once ternmesh.org lists
it, and keeps its saved state:

```bash
tools/companion.py --port <port> update tern-heltec-v3-<region>-0.5.0-alpha.2-app.bin
```

For a new node, or to start a board afresh with a new address, write the full image at `0x0`.
For nRF52840 boards, press RST twice and copy the matching `.uf2` file to the drive that appears.
Never power a board with nothing on the antenna connector. `SHA256SUMS` has the SHA-256 of every
image.

## What to know

* **An update is not signed.** Anyone who has paired with a board can give it firmware, or turn
  it off, as they can change its region.
* **Saved messages and keys are not encrypted**, a group's secret among them. Anyone holding the
  board can read them.
* **A join code is a group's secret.** Share it as you would the group itself.
* **A card is public.** Its name goes to every board within two hops, unsealed.
* **The rules are yours to keep.** The regions follow each country's rules as written, but
  nothing here is certified, and whoever operates a radio answers for it.
