The third alpha of 0.5 runs on a new board, the Seeed SenseCAP T1000-E, and a board now keeps the
cards it has heard when it restarts. It also adds a way to install firmware on an nRF52840 board
without pressing its button.

It is an alpha. The protocol is a draft and will change, and a later build may not talk to this
one or keep its sessions. Nobody outside the project has reviewed the cryptography. Do not rely
on it for anything that matters.

## What changed

* **Seeed SenseCAP T1000-E.** A new nRF52840 board with an LR1110 radio. On a board, it starts,
  makes contact with another node, and both ends acknowledge messages in each direction. It has
  no screen.
* **Cards kept across a restart.** A board saves each card it holds and restores it when it
  starts again. Once the board knows the time, a restored card's age continues from when it was
  last heard, and a card that has passed its time is forgotten. Before this, a restarted board
  knew no names until each neighbour next sent its card, which could take up to three hours.
* **Install without a button on nRF52840 boards.** Opening the companion port at 1200 baud puts
  the board into its bootloader, so a tool or an app can install firmware on it with no button
  pressed.

First contact, sessions, messages, groups, positions, cards and routing are unchanged on the air
from 0.5.0-alpha.2, and so is the companion protocol (version 10). A board on 0.5.0-alpha.2 can be
updated over its companion link and keeps its address, sessions, contacts, groups, messages,
settings and paired phones.

## What was tested, and what was not

CI built every image of this release for every board and region, and ran the firmware test
suite, including new tests for cards kept across a restart. Before the release, the T1000-E was
run against a Heltec T114 and a Heltec V4. The T114 and the V4 also restarted and kept their cards,
with the cards' ages carried over correctly. In one of four installs on the T1000-E, the board did
not start until the install was repeated with its button. The images have not been run on every
physical board as part of this release.

## Updating

A Heltec V3 on 0.2.0-alpha.1 or later takes this release over the link once ternmesh.org lists
it, and keeps its saved state:

```bash
tools/companion.py --port <port> update tern-heltec-v3-<region>-0.5.0-alpha.3-app.bin
```

For a new node, or to start a board afresh with a new address, write the full image at `0x0`.
For nRF52840 boards, put the board in its bootloader, either by pressing RST twice or by opening
its port at 1200 baud, and then copy the matching `.uf2` file to the drive that appears. Never
power a board with nothing on the antenna connector. `SHA256SUMS` has the SHA-256 of every image.

## What to know

* **An update is not signed.** Anyone who has paired with a board can give it firmware, or turn
  it off, as they can change its region.
* **Saved messages and keys are not encrypted**, a group's secret among them. Anyone holding the
  board can read them, and the cards it has heard.
* **A join code is a group's secret.** Share it as you would the group itself.
* **A card is public.** Its name goes to every board within two hops, unsealed.
* **The rules are yours to keep.** The regions follow each country's rules as written, but
  nothing here is certified, and whoever operates a radio answers for it.
* **The classic ESP32 boards are nearly out of memory.** The Heltec V2 and V2.1, LilyGo T-Beam,
  and T3 V1.6.1 have 8 bytes of static RAM to spare in this build.
