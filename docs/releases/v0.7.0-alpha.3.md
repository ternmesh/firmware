The third alpha of 0.7 shows how each message went, direct or through relays and how well it was
heard, adds traces to find the way a node's frames take, makes nodes that sleep easier to reach and
to manage, and runs on the RAK4631.

It is an alpha. The protocol is a draft and will change. Everything said about security in
[0.7.0-alpha.1](v0.7.0-alpha.1.md) still holds, including what is not yet done.

## New

* **How a message went.** For each message received, the node tells the apps how many hops it
  came (1 is straight from the other node) and how well it was heard. For each message sent, it
  tells them the neighbour it went to, how many times it went on the air and, once delivered, how
  the acknowledgement came back and the round trip. A group message's hops are the most it can
  have come. The console's `heard` and `delivered` lines show the same.
* **Traces.** `trace <routing id>` on the console, or in the apps, sends a trace that every node
  on the way adds its routing id and signal to, out and back. A relay with no route on answers it,
  and so does the keeper of a leaf that sleeps, so a trace shows where a message would stop. A
  leaf answers only a node it shares a session with. A trace through a node on an earlier release
  is never answered: those nodes drop it.
* **The RAK4631** (the RAK4630 module on its base board) runs. It has no screen, so it pairs over
  Bluetooth with 123456, as the T1000-E does.

## Fixed

* **Managing a node that is not yours** is refused at once, instead of showing "delivered" and
  then nothing for two minutes.
* **A relay that restarts keeps its sleeping leaves.** Before, it forgot them until each next
  announced, and gave up what it held for them.
* **Managing a sleeping node** takes seconds, not one sleep per request: after an owner's request,
  the node polls its relay every 5 seconds for 2 minutes.
* **A sleeping leaf that restarts** finds its relay again within a minute. Before, it could go
  more than 8 minutes with none.
* **`selftest`** needs a few KB of memory, not 33 KB, and runs on every nRF52840 board.

The companion protocol is now version 18, which Android 0.6.0, Apple 0.7.0 and the web app at
ternmesh.org/app speak. An older client still connects, but sees no hops or signal and cannot
trace.

## What was tested, and what was not

CI built every image for every board and region and ran the firmware tests and the simulator.

On the bench, traces were answered across one hop and across two, through a relay, between
Heltec V3 and V4 boards, and a leaf declined one from a node it had no session with. A RAK4631 exchanged
messages with a T1000-E, acknowledged both ways, and passed `selftest`.

Not yet tried: a drive test across several relays. On a RAK4631 with no battery fitted, the
battery reads 100%, from the charger.

## Updating

As for [0.7.0-alpha.1](v0.7.0-alpha.1.md#updating): over the link with a signed image, over
Bluetooth for an nRF52840 board with the OTAFIX bootloader, or by copying the `.uf2` in its
bootloader. The RAK4631's bootloader is not OTAFIX: double-press its reset button and copy the
`.uf2` to the `RAK4631` drive. Every file is signed by the release key
`09800d79c2c3d0cab7f1710a18cd50d6f0e39c57d9cfe395b371195d4942f88d` and listed in `SIGNATURES`.

```bash
tools/companion.py --port <port> update tern-heltec-v3-<region>-0.7.0-alpha.3-app.bin
```

Never power a board with nothing on the antenna connector.
